### About me

<p align="justify">
I hold a bachelor's degree<sup>1</sup> in <a href="https://geesufmg.com/"> Systems Engineering</a>, a master's degree<sup>2</sup> in Computational Intelligence and a PhD in Optimization, all from <a href="https://ufmg.br/international-visitors"> Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG)</a>, with visiting research experience at the <a href="https://www.bristol.ac.uk/"> University of Bristol</a>. I currently serve as Associate Director of Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence at <a href="https://www.btgpactual.us/">BTG Pactual</a>. With over 10 years of research and development experience, my work spans computer vision, data science, machine learning, natural language processing, operations research and optimization. In my free time, I like training Brazilian jiu jitsu, skateboarding, traveling, cooking and enjoying a good cup of coffee. Feel free to get in touch!
</p>
